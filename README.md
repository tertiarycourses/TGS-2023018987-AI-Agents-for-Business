## About the course

Apply language models, NLP, retrieval, structured tools and human approval to customer support, sales, finance, HR and operations. The course combines visual mechanism-led teaching with detailed guided labs using synthetic business data.

## Learning outcomes

- Evaluate NLP and agent designs for business workflows using architecture, evidence and measurable constraints.
- Prepare text, embeddings and grounded context; build and inspect tool-enabled agent workflows.
- Compare and adapt domain models using reproducible training and held-out evaluation.
- Deploy bounded business-agent workflows with human approval, monitoring and incident recovery.

## Topics covered

- Foundations of AI Agents for Business
- Building and Integrating Business AI Agents
- Intelligent Business Automation with AI Agents
- Deploying, Governing, and Optimising AI Agents

## Public package and usage

- Visual slide deck and PDF, Learner Guide DOCX/PDF/Markdown, and Lesson Plan DOCX/PDF are in `courseware/`.
- Every lab has its own prompt PDF, readable guide, synthetic CSV/JSON inputs, output template and runnable verification scripts.
- Detailed procedures are in the Learner Guide and lab guides. Run commands from the relevant lab folder.
- Labs 2, 3 and 10 use real Hugging Face operations with a small locally initialised model. They teach mechanics and evaluation, not production accuracy. Other scripts use Python's standard library.
- Lab 5 offers a human-relay tool loop and an optional API loop using your own approved model/credentials. No exercise sends messages, releases payments or changes production systems.
- Assessment delivery retains the original 60-minute WA and 90-minute PP timings. The 16-hour plan allocates 13.5 hours to instruction and 2.5 hours to assessment; breaks are additional.
